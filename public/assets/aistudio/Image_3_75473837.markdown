## Brand & Style
The design system positions itself as an intelligent, uplifting academic co-pilot tailored to modern high school and university students. The design movement pairs **Clean Tech-Forward Minimalism** with **Vibrant Micro-Accents** and **Subtle Glassmorphism**, avoiding both clinical academic austerity and juvenile gamification.

The UI must evoke focus, momentum, and intellectual confidence. Visual noise is minimized to reduce cognitive load during late-night study sessions or high-stakes exam review. High contrast, precise layout geometry, and electric accent pops create an interface that feels fast, assistive, and inherently modern.

## Colors
The palette balances cognitive calm with dynamic, high-engagement visual markers:

- **Primary (`#584BF6` - Vibrant Indigo/Violet):** Anchor for core interactive states, high-priority navigation, and brand identification. Expresses intelligence, calm focus, and modern tech capability.
- **Secondary (`#00D4FF` - Electric Cyan):** Used sparingly for AI-generated suggestions, active prompts, progress trackers, and real-time processing indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB300` - Bright Amber):** Dedicated to motivational feedback, streak indicators, mastery badges, and urgent study alerts.
- **Neutral (`#1E1F2E` - Deep Slate/Obsidian):** Supplies high-contrast typography in light mode, structured dark surfaces in dark mode, and eliminates muddy gray scales.

In **Dark Mode**, backgrounds shift to a layered deep indigo-slate (`#0C0D14` surface base, `#161726` elevated card container) while `#584BF6` shifts slightly lighter to `#6D62FF` for accessible legibility.

## Typography
The system creates an intentional pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a friendly, crisp, modern structural rhythm for display headers, callouts, and metric counters, while **Inter** delivers neutral, fatigue-free readability for study summaries, flashcards, and AI dialogue.

Headings feature tight tracking to look punchy and confident on mobile canvases. Body copy maintains generous line-height ratios (1.55 to 1.6) to ensure long-form revision texts and complex STEM definitions remain effortless to parse.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an 8pt spatial baseline using a fluid, content-first grid:
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Tap targets are locked to a strict 44px minimum vertical constraint.
